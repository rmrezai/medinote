# MediNote Pilot Protocol v0.1

## Objective
Evaluate whether MediNote reduces hospitalist documentation/reconciliation burden while maintaining factual fidelity, medication-state accuracy, uncertainty preservation, and physician control.

## Design
Prospective controlled operational pilot with physician review of all output. Adapt this template to local quality-improvement/research, privacy, legal, compliance, and institutional requirements.

## Primary outcomes
- Consequential documentation/medication discrepancies per 100 encounters.
- Physician acceptance/edit burden.
- Documentation workflow time.

## Secondary outcomes
Fact accuracy; medication-state accuracy; certainty preservation; unsupported claims; safety-alert burden/usefulness; module failure rate; user trust.

## Governance
Chart facts → MCIF/Codex → Audit Layer → Physician → Final Epic documentation. MediNote does not independently diagnose, order, prescribe, discharge, or sign.

## Change control
Version clinical rules, prompts, model versions, and audit behavior. Material clinical changes require documentation and regression validation; urgent safety fixes follow incident/change control.

## Oversight
Assign pilot owner, clinical safety lead, technical owner, privacy/security contact, and physician participants.

## Reporting
Daily operational review early in rollout, weekly formal review, and Day-30 report.
