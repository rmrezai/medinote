# MediNote Issue Reporting and Escalation

## Critical
Potential/actual serious patient-safety event, wrong-patient data, material PHI/security exposure, or loss of clinical/audit integrity. Stop affected workflow, preserve evidence, and notify clinical safety plus technical/security leads immediately.

## High
Clinically consequential error caught before harm or repeated failure likely to affect care/documentation. Pause affected feature when appropriate and adjudicate promptly.

## Moderate
Non-immediate clinical/documentation defect requiring correction. Log and include in daily review.

## Low
Usability/cosmetic/nonclinical issue. Normal backlog.

## Minimum report
Timestamp; module/build version; severity; concise de-identified description; expected vs observed behavior; whether caught before finalization; safety-flag behavior; corrective action/status.

Do not paste PHI into a general-purpose issue tracker unless explicitly approved for PHI.
