Why MediNote

Not just note generation. Documentation intelligence.

MediNote is designed around clinical truth, medication-state reconciliation, contradiction detection, and auditability rather than smooth prose alone.

Evidence-linked output

Major documentation claims are anchored to supplied facts, dates, sources, and objective evidence when available.

Medication-state aware

Separates home lists, active orders, MAR administration, held/stopped/resumed states, and discharge intent.

Audit before finalization

Flags unsupported claims, stale data, unresolved contradictions, and discharge-completion risk before copy-to-chart use.

Core workflows

Built for the inpatient documentation loop.

HOOP

Progress notes

Acuity-ordered problems, two-sentence HPI, dated evidence, focused exam, and disposition.

med2

Medication reconciliation

Actionable medication/order addendum with explicit state and objective rationale.

signout

Handoff

One-liner, active problems, pending items, overnight risks, and if/then contingencies.

DCC

Discharge course

Diagnosis-organized discharge narrative with trajectory, medication transitions, pending items, and follow-up needs.

dispo

Disposition reasoning

Current barriers, functional needs, placement issues, equipment, oxygen/infusion needs, and remaining inpatient need.

query risk

CDI review

Neutral query opportunities based on objective indicators, missing specificity, and unable-to-determine options.

Clinical governance

The chart owns the facts. The physician owns the decision.

MediNote organizes, synthesizes, reconciles, and audits. It does not autonomously diagnose, order treatment, reconcile medications, authorize discharge, or replace the treating clinician.

No PHI in public demoUse synthetic or properly de-identified cases only.
