# Founding Customer Agreement — Counsel Drafting Outline

Not legal advice. Use healthcare/privacy counsel for the executed documents.

## Commercial
- Parties and term
- 30-day pilot scope
- Named users/site(s)
- Pilot fee/payment
- No automatic renewal unless explicitly agreed
- Post-pilot option/pricing framework

## Product scope
- Enabled modules
- Physician-supervised use
- No autonomous clinical actions
- No guarantee of clinical, coding, financial, or operational outcomes
- Supported/unsupported workflows

## Data/privacy/security
- Permitted data
- Roles/responsibilities under applicable privacy law
- BAA where required
- Approved subprocessors/model providers
- Retention/deletion
- Security controls
- Incident notification
- Audit logs
- Prohibited data exports/use

## Clinical governance
- Physician remains responsible for decisions/orders/final record
- Safety escalation
- Pilot inclusion/exclusion
- Hard-stop authority
- Customer clinical oversight

## Validation/data rights
- Pilot metrics
- De-identified/aggregate analytics terms
- Product improvement rights only as expressly agreed
- Case-study/reference rights require separate explicit permission

## Legal/risk
- Confidentiality
- IP ownership/license
- Warranties/disclaimers
- Indemnity
- limitation of liability
- insurance
- termination
- data return/deletion
- governing law
