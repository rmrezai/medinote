# Medical Director Pitch

“MediNote is being built specifically around inpatient hospitalist workflow rather than ambient transcription alone.

The system maintains a structured longitudinal patient state across the admission: active problems and trajectories, medications across home/hospital/discharge, consultants, pending studies, disposition, and contradictions. That same state supports H&P, daily progress documentation, discharge, medication reconciliation, and signout.

Every output remains physician-reviewed. MediNote does not independently order, prescribe, diagnose, discharge, or sign. An audit layer checks the draft against the structured state before finalization.

We are looking for a small hospitalist group to run a controlled 30-day founding pilot. The goal is not to ask you to believe an AI claim—we agree on the metrics beforehand and measure whether it actually saves physician time, reduces reconstruction burden, preserves medication and diagnostic state, catches meaningful discrepancies, and earns physician adoption.

If the results are not compelling, you should not buy it.”
