# MediNote ROI Calculator

## Inputs
A = participating physicians
B = shifts per physician/year
C = minutes saved per shift
D = loaded physician labor value/hour
E = MediNote monthly price/physician

## Formulas
Hours saved/physician/year = B × C ÷ 60
Labor value saved/physician/year = Hours saved × D
Annual MediNote cost = A × E × 12
Annual estimated labor value = A × Hours saved × D
Illustrative ROI multiple = Annual estimated labor value ÷ Annual MediNote cost

## Example
A=10
B=182
C=20 minutes
D=$150/hour
E=$299/month

Hours saved/physician/year = 60.67
Illustrative labor value/physician/year = $9,100
Total annual labor value = $91,000
Annual MediNote cost = $35,880
Illustrative value/cost = 2.54×

## Important
Use actual pilot-measured time savings before making customer ROI claims. Do not count coding, safety, throughput, retention, or revenue benefits unless they are separately measured and supportable.
