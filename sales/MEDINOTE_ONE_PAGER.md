# MediNote — Inpatient Intelligence for Hospitalists

## One patient state. Five hospitalist workflows.
MediNote helps hospitalists reconstruct and maintain the clinical story across an admission, then turns that physician-reviewed state into H&P, Progress, Discharge, Medication Reconciliation, and Signout workflows.

### The problem
Hospitalists repeatedly reconstruct the same patient from fragmented labs, medications, consultant recommendations, prior notes, pending studies, therapy recommendations, and disposition updates. Traditional note-generation tools can draft prose, but inpatient care requires longitudinal state, reconciliation, and safe transitions.

### The MediNote workflow
Chart/source data → MCIF structured patient state → Patient Overview → H&P / Progress / Discharge / Med Rec / Signout → Safety Review → Physician approval → Final documentation.

### What makes MediNote different
- Longitudinal inpatient patient state
- Current-vs-historical reconciliation
- Home → Hospital → Discharge medication state
- Problem trajectory and evidence provenance
- Consultant/pending/disposition reconciliation
- Contradiction detection
- Physician-controlled safety audit
- Immutable generation/edit history

### Physician remains in control
MediNote drafts, structures, reconciles, and flags. It does not independently diagnose, order, prescribe, discharge, or sign the medical record.
