# MediNote Proposed Pricing

## Founding pilot
30 days • up to 10 hospitalists • $2,500 flat

## Post-pilot
- Founding customer: $199 / physician / month
- Standard target: $299 / physician / month
- 10+ physicians: volume agreement
- Enterprise / EHR integration: custom annual agreement

## Illustrative annual subscription
5 physicians at $299/mo = $17,940/year
10 physicians = $35,880/year
25 physicians = $89,700/year
50 physicians = $179,400/year

These are proposed launch prices, not guaranteed future pricing. Enterprise pricing should reflect integration, support, security, implementation, analytics, and organizational value rather than simple seat multiplication.
