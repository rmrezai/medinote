# MediNote Founding Hospitalist Pilot Proposal

## Proposed scope
- 30 calendar days
- Up to 10 named hospitalists
- Controlled adult hospital-medicine use
- Enabled workflows: Overview, H&P, Progress, Discharge, Med Rec, Signout, Safety Review
- No autonomous orders, prescribing, discharge, or chart signing

## Proposed fee
$2,500 flat for the 30-day founding pilot.

A design-partner credit or waiver may be negotiated for substantial structured participation, but should not become the default commercial model.

## Included
- Pilot deployment/onboarding support
- Physician training
- Defined inclusion/exclusion criteria
- Issue escalation process
- Usage and validation measurement
- Weekly pilot review
- Day-30 outcome report

## Measures
- Consequential discrepancies per 100 encounters
- Factual accuracy/adjudication
- Medication-state accuracy
- Diagnostic-certainty preservation
- Physician edit ratio
- Workflow time
- Alert usefulness/burden
- Module completion/failure rate
- Physician adoption and qualitative feedback

## Responsibilities
MediNote team: approved deployment, support, monitoring, issue triage, reporting.
Customer: institutional approvals, participating physicians, approved data workflow, clinical oversight, feedback/adjudication.

## End-of-pilot options
GO → convert to subscription.
GO WITH CONDITIONS → extend under agreed mitigations.
HOLD → correct deficiencies before expansion.
STOP → discontinue.

Commercial/legal terms remain subject to executed agreement, privacy/security review, and healthcare counsel.
