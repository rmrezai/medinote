Continuity from
hospital to home.

Our transitional-care model supports adults after a hospital stay, alongside their existing primary care clinician and specialists. The focus is recovery, medication clarity, and maintaining independence at home.

Referral intake and transitional-care visits are not yet open. Please use your current care team for discharge follow-up.

01

Understand the discharge plan

Review discharge instructions, medication changes, pending results, and recommended follow-up.

02

Connect the care team

Coordinate primary care and specialist follow-up, with caregiver participation when the patient permits.

03

Support recovery at home

Plan telemedicine follow-up and review practical barriers to the discharge plan. Clinical concerns require assessment by the responsible care team.
