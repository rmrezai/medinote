About Clinistry

CLINISTRY is a healthcare technology company focused on adult primary care and hospital-to-home recovery through physician-led telemedicine.

Our geriatric focus centers on continuity and independence at home. Transitional care is designed to complement the patient’s existing primary care clinician and specialists.
