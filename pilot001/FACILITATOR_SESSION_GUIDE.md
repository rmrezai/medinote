# Pilot 001 Facilitator Session Guide

## Before participant arrives
- Verify RC v0.1 checksum/release identity.
- Use synthetic/de-identified data only.
- Reset test organization and participant account.
- Confirm system health/readiness.
- Prepare assigned cases and observer form.
- Do not reveal expected answers or hidden safety traps.

## 10-minute onboarding
Explain only:
- MediNote maintains a structured inpatient state.
- Physician remains responsible for all clinical decisions and final documentation.
- Main modules: Overview, H&P, Progress, Med Rec, Signout, Discharge, Safety Review.
- Accept/Edit/Regenerate behavior.
- Safety flags should be investigated, not clicked through simply to proceed.
- A wrong-patient or identity warning is a hard stop.

## Participant tasks
### Task 1 — Orient to patient state
Ask participant to identify:
- highest-acuity current problem;
- current oxygen state;
- one consequential medication transition;
- one pending/disposition issue.

### Task 2 — Generate and review documentation
Use the assigned H&P or Progress workflow. Participant should edit anything they disagree with.

### Task 3 — Medication reconciliation
Ask participant to identify any unresolved or consequential Home → Hospital → Discharge medication state.

### Task 4 — Safety/adjudication
If the case contains a contradiction, ask the physician to resolve it using clinical judgment and available sources.

### Task 5 — Transition workflow
Use Signout and/or Discharge depending on case assignment.

### Task 6 — Finalization
Participant should approve/finalize only after the note reflects their judgment.

## Facilitator rules
- Do not coach ordinary navigation unless the participant is irretrievably stuck.
- Record exact point of confusion before helping.
- Immediately stop for a hard-stop safety condition.
- Do not collect unnecessary PHI or personal clinical details from the participant.

## Closing interview
Ask:
1. What was the most useful part?
2. What did you distrust or have to double-check?
3. What felt slower than your normal workflow?
4. Which module would you actually use first?
5. What would stop you from using MediNote on a shift?
6. Would you use this again on de-identified cases? Why or why not?
